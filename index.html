How we work

More inside than outside.

Outside counsel answers the question you asked. In-house counsel owns the ones you didn't. We're the second kind, before you can hire one. The full argument.

We sit inside

A legal@ address at your company. Advice often shows up before the question does.

We build the process

Stage checklists, an obligations and compliance calendar, your playbook, a diligence-ready data room.

We tell you the truth

Risk assessment adjusted to your stage. When the hours included in your plan run short, we stop and ask before continuing to bill.

We help you hire your full-time GC

When it's time, we help you recruit one, set them up on our tools, and step back into an advisory role.
